Celtic Tree Astrology · Ogham

Discover your Celtic tree sign: which tree represents your birth date?

Celtic Tree Astrology is a popular modern tradition inspired by the ancient Irish connection between trees, nature and the Ogham alphabet, an early form of Irish writing in which many letters are associated with trees.

The popular 13-tree calendar divides the year into thirteen periods, each represented by a different tree. Your date of birth determines your Celtic tree sign, with each tree symbolising different qualities, characteristics and connections with nature.

At our Circle of Life, a Celtic tree astrology circle in the Wicklow Mountains, you can celebrate this connection by sponsoring one of the 13 symbolic trees and having it planted as part of our native woodland project.
