## Brand & Style
The brand embodies an authentic, heritage-rooted synthesis of sustainable Irish woodland stewardship and timeless holiday tradition. The aesthetic is calm, organic, and elevated—evoking crisp Wicklow mornings, hand-harvested spruce, and fireside warmth rather than commercial holiday clutter.

The visual style pairs **Editorial Warmth** with **Refined Natural Tactility**:
- Uncluttered, generous spatial rhythm with clear editorial hierarchy.
- Textural lightness built on pale fibrous paper surfaces rather than stark digital whites.
- Whispered organic line work, delicate dividers, and curated botanical data accents.
- Purposeful high-contrast moments reserved for seasonal action triggers (berries and ribbon).

## Colors
The palette balances deep forest density with airy frost-dappled grounds.

- **Primary (`#1C3A2C` — Deep Spruce):** Commands authority and permanence. Used for major typographic headings, primary containers, dark hero banners, and high-emphasis framing.
- **Secondary (`#5C7A3E` — Moss Forest):** Reflects living foliage and sustainability details. Applied to secondary accents, badges, botanical notes, and subtle selection states.
- **Tertiary (`#A3263A` — Holly Berry):** Vibrant, festive accent used strictly for call-to-action buttons, seasonal badges, notification alerts, and active conversion points.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F3F5EF` — Frost Linen):** Default light surface evoking textured uncoated heritage paper. Reduces digital glare and anchors the earthy palette.
- **Text & Contrast (`#152820` — Slate Evergreen):** The primary reading ink, softer and richer than pitch black, preventing harsh digital contrast while maintaining WCAG AAA accessibility on neutral grounds.

## Typography
Typographic expression bridges 19th-century Irish forestry publications and modern digital clarity:

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys literary stature, heritage, and holiday warmth. Generates strong focal points with tight tracking on larger scales.
- **Body & Functional Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Unassuming, highly readable grotesque that ensures frictionless booking, specification reading, and shopping journeys.
- **Botanical Metadata & Dates (JetBrains Mono):** Set in upper/tabular casing with spaced tracking to emulate scientific forestry tagging, harvest batch numbers, and delivery schedules.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a thoughtful, asymmetric editorial grid inspired by vintage botanical monographs:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum 48px (`3rem`) outer margin. Content maxes out at `1280px` to maintain comfortable eye travel. Editorial feature blocks favor off-center 7:5 and 8:4 column distributions.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px outer canvas margins. Multi-column editorial specs stack naturally into distinct linear cards.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous section spacing (`space-2xl` on desktop, `space-xl` on mobile) ensures content breathes naturally like spaced pine groves.

## Elevation & Depth
This design avoids heavy digital drop shadows and skeuomorphic bevels, adopting **Tonal Layering with Delicate Filigree Outlines**:

- **Layering & Planes:** Depth is achieved by placing pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale evergreen tinted surfaces (`#EAECE4`) atop the `#F3F5EF` canvas.
- **Hairline Outlines:** All elevated containers employ whisper-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(28, 58, 44, 0.12)`).
- **Shadow Signature:** When floating overlays, date-pickers, or carts require separation, use an ultra-diffused, botanical-tinted ambient shadow:
  - *Standard Hover / Card Elevation:* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(21, 40, 32, 0.07)`
  - *Modal / Popover Elevation:* `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(21, 40, 32, 0.14)`
- **Atmospheric Scrims:** Fullscreen backdrops use deep spruce with an 85% opacity blur (`rgba(28, 58, 44, 0.85)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).

## Shapes
A disciplined, understated shape architecture (`Soft / Level 1`):

- Standard components (buttons, text inputs, listing cards) utilize **`4px` (0.25rem)** border radii.
- Selected badges, pill tags, and seasonal notification chips take contextual soft rounding (`rounded-lg` at `8px` or full capsules for botanical chips only).
- Avoid round, bubble-like aesthetic treatments; crisp, architectural edges reflect the structure of trimmed pine branches, book binding, and traditional timber craftsmanship.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (CTA / Reserve Tree):** Holly Berry (`#A3263A`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` radius. Hover state transitions to darkened berry (`#871E2E`) with a subtle `translateY(-1px)`.
- **Secondary (Woodland Stewardship / Details):** Deep Spruce (`#1C3A2C`) solid background with `#F3F5EF` text.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Transparent background, `1px solid #1C3A2C`, `#1C3A2C` text. Hover fills to `rgba(28, 58, 44, 0.05)`.
- **Labeling:** Clean `button-text` tokens; uppercase only for monospaced seasonal triggers.

### Chips & Botanical Tags
- Monospaced text (`label-mono-xs`) set inside compact badges.
- Background: Soft Moss tint (`#EBF0E4`), text: Deep Spruce (`#1C3A2C`), delicate border: `1px solid rgba(92, 122, 62, 0.25)`.
- Used to signify tree height (e.g., `7-8 FT / NORDMANN FIR`), harvest batch, and provenance.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: Clean white (`#FFFFFF`) set over the neutral linen canvas.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(28, 58, 44, 0.2)`, transitioning to `1.5px solid #1C3A2C` on focus.
- Placeholder text: Slate green muted tint (`rgba(21, 40, 32, 0.4)`).
- Form hints and field labels use `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium with optional `JetBrains Mono` format helpers.

### Cards & Tree Catalog Grids
- Clean white rectangular canvases framed with `1px solid rgba(28, 58, 44, 0.08)`.
- Ample inner padding (`space-lg`).
- Image containers feature full-bleed photography of real forestry with warm, desaturated natural light.
- Metadata table embedded in cards displays needle retention, fragrance tier, and cut date in `label-mono-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square markers for checkboxes (`3px` radius) and circles for radio selectors.
- Inactive: White fill with `1.5px solid rgba(28, 58, 44, 0.3)`.
- Active: Deep Spruce (`#1C3A2C`) fill with crisp white checkmark or center pip.

### Botanical Tree Passport & Delivery Tracker
- Custom component acting as a heritage verification receipt.
- Features perforated top border styling, monospaced nursery coordinates, carbon-offset metrics, and scheduled Dublin delivery window.
