Privacy notice

Last updated 8 November 2026.

Who we are

The Original Dublin Christmas Trees is the controller of the details you give us on this site. Contact: theoriginaldublinchristmastree@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

Only what you type into the reservation form: your name, email address, what you are interested in, the tree you chose, and your Dublin area or Eircode if you give it. We use it to reply to you, confirm your reservation, arrange delivery and send your certificate. You choose to send it, and you agree to this use by ticking the box on the form.

Where it is kept

When you press the button, your request is saved in the owner's order list and you are given a reference number. The list is held with Cloudflare, a hosting company, in a database set up to be stored in Europe. Only the owner can open it, with a private key. A copy is also emailed to the owner through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service, so the owner is alerted straight away. Read the privacy policies of both companies on their websites. If the order list cannot be reached, the page uses the email service alone, or opens your own email app with the request written out so that you can press Send.

What this site does not do

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no fonts or scripts loaded from other companies. The only requests to other companies are the ones made when you send the form, to the services named above. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may record technical details such as your IP address in its own server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for that.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to deal with your request and any delivery, and as the law requires for records. The owner erases an order from the list when it is no longer needed or when you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, by emailing theoriginaldublinchristmastree@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ireland at dataprotection.ie.
