Privacy notice

Last updated 8 November 2026.

Who we are

The Original Dublin Christmas Trees is the controller of the details you give us on this site. Contact: romacsweeney@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

Only what you type into the reservation form: your name, email address, what you are interested in, the tree you chose, and your Dublin area or Eircode if you give it. We use it to reply to you, confirm your reservation, arrange delivery and send your certificate. You choose to send it, and you agree to this use by ticking the box on the form.

How it reaches us

The form opens your own email app with your request already written, and you press Send. The details travel by email to the address above and are kept in that mailbox. If the site is later connected to a form service, this notice will name it before it is used.

What this site does not do

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no fonts or scripts loaded from other companies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may record technical details such as your IP address in its own server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for that.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to deal with your request and any delivery, and as the law requires for records.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, by emailing romacsweeney@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ireland at dataprotection.ie.
